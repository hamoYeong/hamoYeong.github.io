whatITried:
  - Started with cognitive load, scaffolding, and metacognition to explore an experience that elicits thought instead of supplying an answer.
  - Defined the thinking actions a learner should perform before selecting syntax, then worked backward to the minimum Swift concepts required.
  - Separated the knowledge and learning systems, structured them in Obsidian, and connected them to tickets and a macOS UI/UX.
  - Used AI as a learning guide in unfamiliar domains and as a way to inspect the gap between thought and result during implementation.
whatChanged:
  - The question changed from “What should I teach?” to “What kind of thinking should the learner practice?”
  - I learned that the quality of the result changed when I revised my question once more and checked the evidence again, rather than simply accepting an AI answer.
learnings:
  - The answer to one question can make the next question more precise instead of finishing the work.
